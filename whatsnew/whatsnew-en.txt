Nexus Game Booster 2.2.0 — account numbers & personal keys!

What's new:
• A unique User-XXXXX account number for your installation: issue it from the login screen or Settings (once per install) and see it in Settings with a copy button.
• Personal keys in the User-XXXXX_XXXXX format: they only work on the issuing account; ownership is validated server-side.
• Separate expiry messages for public/personal/test keys; an in-app notice appears when a key expires mid-use and validity is re-checked every 30 seconds.
• Admin panel and Worker updated (the D1 migration must be run once — see the deploy guide).
• Rocket Edition look unchanged; nothing removed and all 115 unit tests stay green.
• Installs directly over 2.1.0 and any older version — same signature, no uninstall needed.
