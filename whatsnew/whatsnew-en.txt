Nexus Game Booster — new version!

What's new:
• A live license countdown now sits on the Home screen: days, hours, minutes and seconds remaining on your access key, with a glowing ring that shows how much of the term is left. Green = active; red = expired or 3 days or less remaining. The app reads the REAL expiry time from the server — nothing is guessed on the device.
• The whole app wears the new NEON ARENA look from the design kit: cut-corner buttons, state-colored tunnel buttons (grey = locked, green = ready, striped = connecting, red = connected/tap to cut), glowing status chips and the retuned deep-navy palette.
• Updating from 0.11.3 installs directly — no uninstall needed (same signature).

Note for the server admin: deploy the updated Worker file (cloudflare-worker/nexus-gamebooster-api.js in the repository) so the server provides the license timestamps. Existing sessions pick the expiry up automatically on the next sync; brand-new activations require the updated Worker first.

Still inside the app: the "Check for updates" button in Settings, plain-language release notes, and one-tap download & install.
