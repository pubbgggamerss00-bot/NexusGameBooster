Nexus Game Booster — new version!

What's new:
• The app now connects to your Nexus panel: sign in once with your access key and everything syncs automatically.
• Your panel's DNS list appears in the DNS changer — pick one and connect, no test needed first.
• WireGuard profiles from your panel are imported and kept up to date for you.
• A manual refresh button plus automatic refresh every five minutes on the Ping Good screen.
• If the server can't be reached, one tap retries — you won't be signed out.

Important: this version is signed with a new key, so you must uninstall the old version once before installing this one. That step also resets in-app data.

Still inside the app: the "Check for updates" button in Settings, plain-language release notes, and one-tap download & install — everything stays in the app.
