Nexus Game Booster 2.0.0 — Arcade UI on the 1.2.0 base

Built exactly as requested: the source and behavior are precisely the 1.2.0 codebase; only the theme, UI and animations come from the reference design (Nexus Game Booster.html). No feature was added or removed — every screen, button and flow matches 1.2.0.

What's new (visuals & motion only):
• Every screen fully restyled in the reference's arcade look: chamfered neon panels with gradient edges, 3D lip buttons with sweeping shine, HUD gauges, segmented keys and skewed stat bars.
• Arcade font kit: Vazirmatn (body), Rajdhani (HUD numbers), Orbitron (tags) — full Persian support.
• Home: floating rocket hero, license-key countdown card with ticking seconds, Shizuku status card, and the REACTOR boost button — orbiting ring, tick dial, hex core with 3D press feel, boost step badges and a particle burst on completion.
• Boost result: bottom sheet with a score ring and live count-up of freed megabytes.
• Library: segmented tabs, arcade search field, skewed colored covers with shine sweep and staggered card entrance.
• Monitor: ring gauges (RAM/battery/temp/cores), live trend charts and a LIVE badge.
• Profiles: selected card scales up with a neon halo and staggered animated stat bars.
• Better ping: ring-style power button for WireGuard, colored signal bars per DNS and a stepped test-result reveal.
• Settings: segmented language & appearance keys (navy/AMOLED), reduce-motion switch and arcade buttons.
• License gate: floating rocket, focus-glow key field and full-width arcade button.
• Bottom nav: sliding chamfered indicator, icons that pop on selection, plus the route-change scan sweep.
• All animations respect the in-app "reduce motion" setting and the system animation scale.

Install: signed with the same permanent key as previous releases — 2.0.0 installs as a normal update over 1.2.2 (and 1.2.1).

Version: 2.0.0 (versionCode 30)
