Nexus Game Booster 2.1.0 (Rocket Edition UI on 1.2.0) — new look!

What's new:
• New rocket app icon: the booster now wears the green rocket badge on your home screen and in the launcher.
• New 3D launch splash: a short rocket-launch animation (starfield, orbit rings, engine flare, arcade power meter) plays while the app opens — purely visual, nothing loads slower and it can be turned off with Reduce motion.
• Full Rocket Edition retheme: the whole interface moved to the new forest-green + neon green/lime palette — surfaces, cards, buttons, the license countdown and the AMOLED black variant included.
• No functional changes: boost engine, profiles, monitoring, library, DNS/WireGuard tools, tickets and settings behave exactly like 1.2.0. No new permissions; 115 unit tests still green.
• Updating from 2.0.0 and any older version installs directly — no uninstall needed (same signature).
